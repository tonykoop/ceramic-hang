# Image-Gen Prompts

Concept image only; not fabrication authority. Do not include cut-ready dimensions, hole locations, or toolpaths.

## Hero Prompt

A slip-cast ceramic handpan-inspired tonal vessel photographed as a finished physical object: a shallow, approachable saucer-domed shell roughly the size of a large handpan, with a clearly raised center ding island at the crown and a ring of smaller raised, isolated oval tone fields arranged around it on the upper dome. The form is unmistakably ceramic rather than steel — a stoneware/porcelain body with the soft matte sheen of an exterior glaze or a burnished oxide surface, subtle clay warmth and faint slip-cast surface character, rounded relief boundaries framing each tone field. The lower shell curves gently to a rounded rim seam where upper and lower cast halves join. Scale reads as a lap-held instrument resting on a low neutral surface, played from above with the hands. Clean neutral studio background, soft directional studio light grazing across the raised fields to reveal their relief, 3/4 product-photography angle, ~50mm lens, high detail, photorealistic, no text labels.

## In-Context Prompt

The ceramic tonal vessel staged in a working ceramics studio: resting on a wooden drying cradle or padded benchtop near a plaster mold and a small array of fired tone-test coupons, warm natural window light from one side, dusty studio atmosphere, a tuner and small microphone just visible in the soft background suggesting acoustic measurement. Shallow depth of field with the raised tone fields and center ding in sharp focus and the studio receding into bokeh. A pair of hands hovers above the dome as if about to strike a field. Environmental storytelling of a research build, concept visualization only, photorealistic, no text labels.

## Detail Prompt

Macro close-up of a single raised oval ceramic tone field on the upper dome: the gentle rise of the cast island, the rounded relief boundary that isolates it from the surrounding shell, the matte glazed or burnished ceramic texture with fine slip-cast surface micro-character, and the subtle thinning at the field crown that acts as the pitch lever. Raking low-angle light to throw the relief edge and field curvature into sharp shadow, extreme detail on the clay surface and glaze, shallow depth of field, photorealistic, no measurements.

## Negative / Avoid

No text, logos, watermarks, or fake maker marks; no dimension lines or measurement callouts; no hammered-steel handpan texture or metallic sheen; no impossible mechanisms; anatomically plausible hands if a player is shown.
